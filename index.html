01

The work stays manual because the control model is missing

A small operator carries the same paperwork as a large one, and none of the back office.

Invoices, receipts, referrals, claims and correspondence arrive unstructured and get keyed in by hand, late, by whoever has time.

General purpose AI would solve most of it. But the people with the worst paperwork burden are usually the ones who are personally accountable for the data in it, and sending client or patient records to an outside model is exactly what they cannot casually do.

So the work stays manual. Not because the technology is missing, but because the control model is.

02

How it works

Frontier model quality on the outside. Your identifiers never make the trip. The mapping between the two stays in your environment.

  1. De-identify

    Direct identifiers are detected and replaced with stable tokens before anything is sent anywhere. The mapping stays inside your environment.

  2. Reason

    Agents extract the fields, reconcile the document against your records, and explain any discrepancy in plain business language.

  3. Propose

    Values are restored locally and a completed draft goes to a review queue with a confidence score per field. A person commits it. Never the agent.

03

The control model is the product

Not a policy page attached to it. Every control below is enforced in the system, visible to you, and evidenced in the audit record.

  • Human in the loop by default

    The system proposes. A person commits.

  • Confidence thresholds with abstention

    Below the line it asks rather than guesses.

  • Fails closed

    If detection is uncertain, the document does not proceed.

  • Least privilege

    Scoped tool access and segregation of duties on anything financial.

  • Versioned, visible policy

    Thresholds and rules are yours to read and change.

  • Complete audit evidence

    Every extraction, score, policy applied and human override.

  • Kill switch

    Suspend an agent, a workflow, a counterparty or an action type.

A claim we will not make. Because the token mapping is reversible, this is pseudonymization rather than anonymization. It reduces exposure. It does not remove your obligations, and we will not tell you otherwise.
