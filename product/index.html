Product

Cloisa drafts the coordinated file. Your preparer approves it.

Cloisa is agentic AI for the coordinated Canada–US file. The agents do the drafting; the professional stays in charge. The output is a draft T1 and 1040 package with a workpaper: every figure placed, every treatment cited, every open question flagged for the reviewer.

01

Four steps, each one visible

  1. Read

    Cloisa reads the client's slips, statements and prior returns from both countries: T4s, T5s and T3s on the Canadian side, W-2s and 1099s on the US side, plus the account statements behind them.

  2. Map

    Each figure is placed where it belongs on the T1 and the 1040, and traced to the exact page it came from. A reviewer can always see the source next to the number.

  3. Reconcile

    Each figure is checked for its treatment under both tax codes and the treaty. The draft explains what it did and cites the authority, so the reviewer checks a position, not a guess.

  4. Approve

    Your preparer works through a review queue, field by field, and commits each one. Anything below the confidence line is asked about, not guessed at. Nothing is committed by the agent.

The result: a draft coordinated file with every figure linked to the document it came from, ready for the firm's own review and sign-off.

02

What Cloisa is not

Clear boundaries are part of the product.

  • Not consumer tax software

    Cloisa is built for professional preparers and the firms they work in, not for filers doing their own returns.

  • Not a research chatbot

    The output is a drafted file with citations, not an answer to a question. Research tools exist; the drafting work is what stays manual today.

  • It does not file

    Nothing goes to the CRA or the IRS from Cloisa. The preparer reviews, the preparer signs, the firm files. Always.
